# AstraCode Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AstraCode community welcoming, safe,
and equitable for everyone. We value contributions made in good faith and
respect the dignity, rights, perspectives, and experiences of all community
members.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- engage with others respectfully, kindly, and honestly;
- respect different viewpoints, experiences, identities, and skill levels;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- take responsibility for their words, actions, and technical contributions;
- acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the
  experience;
- respect the project's purpose, technical boundaries, and community spaces;
- protect confidential or private information; and
- credit the work and ideas of others appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is unacceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome personal attention;
- insulting, demeaning, discriminatory, or stereotyped comments or conduct;
- sexualized language, imagery, or attention inappropriate to the community;
- publishing or using another person's private information without permission;
- advocating or threatening violence or other harm;
- impersonation or evasion of a prior moderation action;
- sustained disruption of technical or community discussions;
- deliberately misleading claims or failure to credit contributed work; and
- other conduct that a reasonable person would consider harmful to the safety
  or well-being of the community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.
They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary
to protect the community.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and impartially as practical. A
maintainer involved in an incident should not decide its outcome when another
uninvolved maintainer is available. Details about a reporter or affected person
will not be shared beyond what is necessary to investigate, protect
participants, and carry out an enforcement decision.

## Reporting an issue

Report possible Code of Conduct violations privately to a repository maintainer
using a private contact method that the maintainer publishes on their GitHub
profile. Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue, discussion,
or pull request.

The repository does not currently publish a dedicated conduct email address. If
no private maintainer contact method is available, open only a minimal public
request asking a maintainer to establish a private channel; do not identify the
people involved or describe the incident publicly.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and other AstraCode community
spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project, such as
using an official account, speaking on behalf of the project, or participating
as an appointed project representative.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, impact, and pattern of behavior
when choosing a response. Depending on the circumstances, they may skip steps
in this guideline:

1. **Warning** — a private explanation of the violation and the behavior
   expected going forward.
2. **Temporary limitation** — a time-limited restriction on particular
   interactions or community activities.
3. **Temporary suspension** — removal from community interaction for a defined
   period, with conditions for returning where appropriate.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal from project community spaces for a
   severe violation or a pattern that prior measures did not resolve.

Where safe and appropriate, maintainers may ask for an acknowledgement,
apology, correction, or other action intended to repair harm. Enforcement is a
community-safety measure, not a public punishment process.
